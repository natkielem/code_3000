## HW 9 - Personal Ethics Statement

I believe that we are responsible for the effects of the technology we design. Thus, ethics dictates that it's necessary to anticipate any possible real-world consequences (whether minor or catastrophic) and act appropriately to reduce the likelihood and severity of harm. This requires precise explanations of ethical values and goals and how they apply to your organization specifically. Further, we should maintain an ongoing commitment to ethical practice, even when doing so creates further challenges.
